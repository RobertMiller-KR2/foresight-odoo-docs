How the work moves

Purchasing brings goods into stock. Sales creates the customer commitment and starts the outbound work. Select a step to jump to its guide.

Outbound · fulfill the customer order

  1. SalesQuotePrice the request
  2. SalesSales orderConfirm acceptance
  3. BarcodePick ticketScan items from bins
  4. BarcodeShippingValidate delivery

The exact handoff depends on the warehouse route and stock availability. A confirmed PO normally generates a receipt; a confirmed sales order can generate a pick and delivery. Finish each Barcode operation only after its actual quantities and destination are correct.

01 · Purchasing

Create a purchase order

  1. Open Purchase → Requests for Quotation and select New.
  2. Choose the vendor, add products and quantities, then check price, expected arrival, and Deliver To.
  3. Save and send the RFQ for vendor review when needed. Select Confirm Order once the purchase is approved.
  4. Open the receipt linked to the PO when the goods arrive.

02 · Barcode receiving

Receive goods

  1. Open Barcode → Operations → Receipts for the correct warehouse.
  2. Scan the receipt barcode or select the receipt from the queue. Match it to the PO and physical delivery.
  3. Scan each product and enter the quantity actually received. Record any shortage or exception shown on the transfer.
  4. When the warehouse requires pricing approval, select Validate → Record Physical Arrival. With QZ SATO Labels 19.0.1.0.8 or newer, enter label quantities and select Print Labels or Skip Printing.
  5. Physical arrival and printing labels do not make stock available. An authorized reviewer confirms pricing, then the receipt is finally validated. Without the pricing workflow, validate normally after checking quantities.
  6. Use Daily Received Items or Weekly Received Items to review Final Validation and Pricing. Select Open to Validate for a pending receipt. Finish any separate Storage transfer afterward.

03 · Barcode storage

Put received goods in stock

  1. Open Barcode → Operations → Storage and select the ready transfer created for putaway.
  2. Scan the product and its destination bin or location as prompted.
  3. Check the quantity moved to that location. Repeat for every line and destination.
  4. Select Validate when the putaway is physically complete.

04 · Sales

Create a customer quote

  1. Open Sales → Orders → Quotations and select New.
  2. Choose the customer and add product lines, quantities, and prices.
  3. Check delivery address, customer PO number if supplied, taxes, terms, and total.
  4. Save, preview, and send the quotation when it is ready for the customer.

For Program quotes, use the detailed quotation SOP to link the Program correctly.

05 · Sales

Confirm a sales order

  1. Open the accepted quotation in Sales → Orders → Quotations.
  2. Recheck customer, products, quantities, prices, delivery address, and promised date.
  3. Select Confirm. Odoo changes the quotation to a sales order.
  4. Open its delivery or warehouse transfer and check the next operation assigned by the route.

06 · Barcode picking

Complete a pick ticket

  1. Open Barcode → Operations → Pick for the correct warehouse.
  2. Scan the pick ticket or select its ready card. Check the transfer reference.
  3. Scan the source bin and each product; enter the quantity actually picked.
  4. Move goods to the destination shown on the ticket and select Validate when the pick is complete.

07 · Barcode shipping

Ship the customer order

  1. Open Barcode → Operations → Delivery Orders for the correct warehouse.
  2. Scan the delivery transfer or select it from the ready queue. Confirm the destination and items match the shipment.
  3. Scan products or packages as prompted and verify the quantity being shipped.
  4. When the goods have been handed off and the details are correct, select Validate to complete the delivery.
